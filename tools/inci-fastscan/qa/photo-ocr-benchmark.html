CER = character edit distance / transcribed ingredient characters after NFKC, case, whitespace and ingredient-delimiter normalization (commas and line breaks compare equivalently; numeric locant commas remain part of names). Ingredient recall/precision require exact spelling (no automatic OCR correction), and preserve repeated ingredients as separate occurrences. Lower CER and higher exact recall are better. These metrics are for the selected photo only, not a universal OCR accuracy claim.
