Screenshot Canvas usage

Add images, drag to move, resize using the corner handles, Shift-click to select multiple, arrange with layout buttons, and export PNG.

Keyboard editing

Choose an image from the Image layers buttons to focus the canvas. Use arrow keys to move by 1px, Shift+arrow by 10px, Delete to remove, Escape to deselect, and Ctrl/Command+Z to undo.

Images are composed locally in your browser and multi-file selection order is preserved. The exported PNG is downloaded locally.

File-size and memory limits

Canvas width and height must each be between 100 and 12,000 pixels, with no more than 24 million pixels total. Each image file must be 32 MiB or smaller and have at most 24 million source pixels; combined layers are limited to 48 million source pixels. Oversized or corrupt files are skipped without losing the current composition. Export may still fail on devices with limited memory.
