## Status semantics

- **PASS:** implementation evidence establishes the current contract, with no hard compliance gap or unresolved product decision; recommendations and missing behavior tests remain separately visible.
- **FIX:** at least one hard implementation/common-spec gap and no higher-precedence unresolved decision.
- **BLOCKED:** registered implementation evidence is unavailable.
- **NEEDS_DECISION:** two or more plausible product behaviors remain after inspecting implementation, documentation, and tests. Missing prose alone never creates this state.
